### 3.1 The outcome in words

Registered outcome: **`error_direction_not_supported`**.

> K15 fails: error-directed routing is not supported at this scale. WS3's central bet is recorded as a negative result, no bank scaling follows, and the mechanism is not carried into WS4/WS5 without a new pre-registration.

### 3.2 What was not measured, and what would change the reading

- **One seed per arm.** No seed-level CI is claimed. The replication in
  A8's arithmetic was not run, so the `H*`-`H` difference is held to T2.8's
  twice-the-item-level-half-width gate, applied in the ledger below.
- **The compact training target.** Evaluation reports the same top-16 + tail
  target the arms trained on. The full-vocabulary comparison is the P2.4
  instrument check (A9) and is not a second quality endpoint.
- **Measured bytes/token.** No I/O counter exists on this instrument; every
  byte figure is modelled and labelled as such.
- **The deferred arms.** `D_M` (equal-total-capacity dense, K17) and `HL`
  (hard-label control, A9) are registered and not run.
- **One GPU, one data order.** Every arm consumed the same token stream in
  the same order at the same budget, which is what makes the pairing valid.
