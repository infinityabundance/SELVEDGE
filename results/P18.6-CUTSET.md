## 12. Honest limits

- The instrument is a **degraded, non-deployed selection regime**: a dense teacher
  with static magnitude masks, not a trained bank. Early-layer KL values are far
  outside the usable tier, so the ε test is not a capability test.
- 8 calibration windows x 5 layers x 4 fixed pairs is not a census; unmeasured
  pairs cannot be bounded. No code or math item was measured.
- The discovery/replication split is internal to calibration text. No result here
  has replicated on the held-out corpus.
- The trained banks X and H have no combination measurements; H* has only
  single-expert labels. Nothing here may be extrapolated to the trained models.
- The coverage simulation is over the stored design, not over the group-pair space;
  its sample-size limits are printed with the tables.
