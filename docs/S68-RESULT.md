## 3. Depth-ramped interpolation (calibration only, then one test pass)

Registration: `docs/s68-depth-ramp-preregistration-v1.json`. The file carries a
`retrospective` label: it was first committed at `2026-11-21T03:12:54Z`, thirty
seconds after the builder receipt that names it (`2026-11-21T03:12:24Z`). The
label quotes that ordering and its evidence, and the `prereg_provenance` court
tolerates it only because the verdict records that the ramp did not beat the
soup champion and changed no selection (§3.2 below). The registration's
substantive content is unchanged; the label is appended, not substituted. The
ramp is

    alpha(l) = alpha_shallow + (alpha_deep - alpha_shallow) * l / (L - 1)

over the `layers.<l>.` keys, with `L = 24` read from the model config. The
final `norm.weight` has no layer index and is mixed at `alpha_deep`; the
receipt lists it. The update is the S67 soup delta, mixed in float32 and
rounded back to bf16.

The builder was checked before any scoring: the degenerate arm
`alpha_shallow = alpha_deep = 0.375` reproduces the S67 constant-alpha soup
checkpoint **tensor for tensor** (`results/stage2/s68-ramp/equivalence.json`).

### 3.2 Test pass (scored once, after the selection was locked)

Against the post-trained 0.8B, paired problem-level bootstrap, 4,000 resamples,
seed 53, flexible basis:

| task | n | post-trained | ramp | delta | 95% interval | above zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSM8K | 1,319 | 54.21% | 63.15% | +8.95 | [+6.22,+11.68] | yes |
| MATH-500 | 500 | 38.20% | 40.60% | +2.40 | [-1.60,+6.40] | no |
| full MATH | 5,000 | 39.60% | 42.16% | +2.56 | [+1.26,+3.84] | yes |

Retention also passes: full MMLU 51.68% against 50.48% (delta +1.20 points,
interval above zero), and MMLU-Pro 4,000 26.45% against 23.43% (delta +3.03
points, interval above zero).

But the question the ramp was built to answer is whether it improves on the
**champion**, the constant-alpha soup. It does not:

| task | ramp | S67 soup | ramp − soup | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSM8K | 63.15% | 63.38% | -0.23 | [-2.65,+2.20] |
| MATH-500 | 40.60% | 40.60% | 0.00 | [-3.80,+3.80] |
| full MATH | 42.16% | 42.12% | +0.04 | [-1.18,+1.30] |

All three intervals include zero. **The depth-ramp calibration gain did not
translate into a test-level improvement.** The constant-alpha soup remains the
champion: it is the simpler model, it already carries the registered claim, and
the ramp is statistically indistinguishable from it. The ramp's own single-run
result also meets the post-trained comparison and retention, but that is a
statement about the ramp against the baseline, not a reason to replace the
soup.

## 4. External frontier position check

Both ungated checkpoints were fetched at pinned revisions, with hashes and
licences recorded in `results/models/s68-frontier-fetch.json` and
`results/models/s68-frontier-inventory.json`. `facebook/MobileLLM-R1.5-950M` is
gated (manual); its file endpoint returned HTTP 401 and it was **not fetched**.

| model | licence | GSM8K (n=1,319) | MATH-500 (n=500) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SELVEDGE champion (S67 soup, 0.8B base) | Apache-2.0 (Qwen3.5-0.8B) | 63.38% | 40.60% |
| post-trained Qwen3.5-0.8B (baseline) | Apache-2.0 | 54.21% | 38.20% |
| Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B | Apache-2.0 | 61.26% | 48.80% |
| amd/ReasonLite-0.6B | OpenRAIL-D | 30.93% | 36.00% |
| facebook/MobileLLM-R1.5-950M | FAIR noncommercial research | not scored (gated, 401) | not scored |

Protocol, identical in intent: greedy, non-thinking, zero-shot CoT, the S54
user template, `max_new_tokens = 4096`, the S54 strict/flexible/exact
extraction, transformers bf16 on one RTX 4080 SUPER. The external 0.6B models
use full attention and hit a CUDA OOM at the champion's batch size; they were
scored at a smaller batch size, which is an execution parameter, not a decoding
change. The champion and baseline rows were scored at the S67/S54 batch size.
The exact values are recorded in the inventory and in each S54 receipt.

**This is our instrument only.** Published vendor numbers for these
checkpoints use different prompts, shot counts, decoding, sampling and answer
extraction; nothing in this table reproduces or contradicts a vendor claim, and
it supports no frontier-leadership statement. The honest reading is narrow:
under our protocol the ungated 0.6B Qwen3 checkpoint scores higher than our
soup on MATH-500 and lower on GSM8K; ReasonLite scores below both; the gated
checkpoint is unmeasured.

Receipt: `results/stage2/s68-frontier/summary.json`.

## 5. Preference run

No preference run was started. The existing S64 preference arm was launched
from the post-trained baseline, not from a champion, and its recorded loss
trajectory collapses to zero on degenerate pairs; it is not a valid
champion-initialised comparison under the fixed trainer. Starting one would
have displaced the depth-ramp and frontier GPU work, and the S68 evidence does
not depend on it. If one is started, the fixed trainer (zero-weight pairs
dropped, CPU-side merge export) must initialise from the constant-alpha soup and
compare against that champion.

## 6. What this is not

* It is not a new architecture claim; the method is weight interpolation.
* It is not a frontier-leadership claim; the external table is our instrument
  and the strongest external row is not the gated checkpoint.
* It is not a replacement of the registered champion. The ramp lost its
  calibration gain on the test and is indistinguishable from the soup.
* The full-MATH instrument was scored once for the ramp and was not enlarged or
  re-scored.
